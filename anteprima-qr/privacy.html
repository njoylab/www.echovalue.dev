Anteprima QR Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Anteprima QR is an iPhone and iPad app published by nJoyLab (IT12822520966). It shows what a QR code contains before you open it.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Anteprima QR app only. echoValue APIs and this website have a separate policy at echoValue privacy.

Data the app collects

Anteprima QR does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not send QR contents, camera frames, or photos to nJoyLab or to any other server.

Camera, photos, and clipboard

The camera is used on the device to read a QR code. Frames are not saved and are not uploaded. If you pick a photo, the code is decoded on the device and the photo is not uploaded.

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Incolla. It writes to the clipboard only when you tap Copia contenuto.

Recent scans on your device

QR codes you have read stay on that device, inside the app, until you delete them. You can delete one entry or clear the list from Letti di recente. Deleting the app deletes the list. We do not keep a copy, so there is no server record to erase. Deleting those entries, or deleting the app, is how you remove the only data the app stores.

Opening a link

If you tap the button to open a link, you leave Anteprima QR. Safari, Mail, Phone, or Messages then handles the address under that app’s own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not share data from this app with third parties. The app does not transmit any.

Children

Anteprima QR is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page and the date above changes.
